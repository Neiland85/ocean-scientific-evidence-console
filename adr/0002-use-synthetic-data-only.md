# ADR-0002 — Use Synthetic Data Only

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The mockup is developed under NDA and must not expose real Ocean Ecostructures operational, scientific, client, location or internal information.

## Decision

All data used in the mockup will be synthetic demo data.

Synthetic data may include fictional installations, monitoring events, biodiversity observations, evidence references, sensor readings, reviewers, uncertainty notes and export reports.

## Consequences

The mockup can demonstrate product flow, scientific traceability and export logic without creating confidentiality, privacy or scientific misrepresentation risks.
